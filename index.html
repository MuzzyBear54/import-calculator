These amounts are additional charges paid on import and do not include the product and shipping cost themselves. EMS thresholds follow the USD product price; DHL uses the USD product price for the exemption and USD CIF (product + shipping) for subsequent brackets. Formula applied exactly as supplied, not independently verified current Israeli fee schedules. Automatic customs conversion uses the usual previous-Friday rate plus 0.5%; it does not account for exceptional customs rules (including a cumulative 3% exchange-rate movement) or the exact date a future parcel is cleared. Other charges (if applicable) are excluded.
