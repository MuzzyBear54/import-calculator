3. Optional import charges

Select only when applicable

Expand a carrier to add conditional destination-side fees. All are initially off. Storage takes total days since arrival, not just billable days. Government certificate costs not specified by the carriers can be entered manually.

These fees are not transportation surcharges (e.g., bulky package, fuel, volumetric weight). Enter all carrier transportation surcharges within your shipping quotation, not here. FedEx special clearance replaces its normal clearance charge rather than being added twice. Sources and limitations appear under each carrier.

Estimated import charges

Enter your product and shipping prices to calculate. Exchange rates load automatically when online.

These are estimated destination-side import charges, excluding the product and transportation prices. EMS and DHL baseline calculations retain the user-supplied models, except the tax-exempt EMS clearance fee of ₪35.40. FedEx follows its published personal-import tariff and uses CIF as the fee-bracket customs value and product price for the tax exemption. Listed special fees are conditional; some can replace other fees, and permit/certificate costs may be additional. DHL optional-fee VAT is enabled by default but can be disabled to match the invoice. Do not assume every listed fee applies. EMS special-services and storage information come from a different/later general-post schedule and are marked as provisional. Automatic customs conversion is an estimate (previous-Friday BOI reference +0.5%) and may differ from the rate at clearance. VAT-exempt status does not mean clearance-service fees are necessarily waived. Excludes goods-specific customs duties or purchase tax.
